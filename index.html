Auto follows your device: Day in light mode, Night in dark mode. You can also put one of your own photos behind the news.

Pick your topics

A town, a team, a fish, a hobby. One or two words finds the most. Press Add after each one.

Getting more out of keywords
  • Plain words: with up to three, every word must be in the story. A place like PNG, NT or Qld is never skipped. Lake Murray PNG
  • Either-or: put a slash between them. yellowcheek / yellow cheek
  • Must have both: put a comma between them. Lake Murray, PNG / Papua
  • Give it a short name: start with the name and a colon. NT grants: NT, grant / funding
  • Float some to the top without requiring the word: square brackets. fly tying [streamer]
  • Leave some out: a minus sign. Bensbach, -Road
  • Australian stories only: add Australia as a must-have. F-150, Australia
  • Quiet topics look back further on their own: a week, then a month, then a year.

Teaching it your taste

On your feed, swipe a story left to hide it, or right for "more like this", "less like this" and "wrong topic". The ⋯ button on each story does the same. The app learns what you like, all on this device.

Publishers blocked from a topic

Tap Wrong topic on a story and its publisher stops showing in that one topic. It still shows in your other topics. Tap a name below to let it back in.

Reading voice

Used by the read-aloud button. Voices come from your device.
